🔑 关键问题
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

❌ MLX `ConvTranspose1d` 缺少 `groups` 参数

影响：
- BigVGAN的anti-aliasing需要depthwise conv_transpose
- 每个AMPBlock有6个activation layers
- 总共108个activation layers需要depthwise操作
- 手动实现需要循环768次/层

性能影响：
- PyTorch MPS: 2.3秒/音频
- MLX手动实现: >10秒/音频 (预估)
- 性能倒退: >4倍


📊 MLX官方功能检查
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✅ 已有功能:
  - mlx.nn.Conv1d (with groups) ✅
  - mlx.nn.ConvTranspose1d ⚠️ (无groups)
  - mlx.nn.Upsample ⚠️ (简单插值，无anti-aliasing)

❌ 缺失功能:
  - ConvTranspose with groups 🔑 关键缺失
  - Anti-aliasing filters (需自实现)
  - Depthwise conv_transpose (需手动循环)


🧪 实现和测试
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✅ 已实现（基础版）:
  1. MLXSnake/MLXSnakeBeta激活函数
     - 精度：diff < 1e-6 ✅
  
  2. MLXAMPBlock (无anti-aliasing)
     - 精度：correlation = 0.87 ⚠️
  
  3. Conv1d/ConvTranspose1d权重转换
     - Conv1d: (O,I,K) → (O,K,I)
     - ConvTranspose1d: (I,O,K) → (O,K,I)
     - 精度：diff < 1e-5 ✅

⚠️ 部分实现（完整版）:
  4. Kaiser sinc filter
  5. LowPassFilter1d (depthwise conv) ✅
  6. UpSample1d (depthwise conv_transpose) ❌ 性能差

测试结果:
  - 单层Conv: diff < 1e-5 ✅
  - 单层Activation: diff < 1e-6 ✅
  - 完整模型: correlation = 0.07 ❌ (anti-aliasing不一致)
